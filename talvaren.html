Talvaren

Talvaren is an original fantasy MMORPG in active development by Talvaren Studios. The game is built around exploration, meaningful character choices, persistent world systems, distinct classes and races, player housing, crafting, discovery, and a continent designed to grow over time.

The world of Talvaren is being developed as a seamless, large-scale environment with hand-shaped terrain, natural regions, hidden paths, mountains, islands, forests, rivers, settlements, dungeons, and the Vast Below. Its systems are designed to reward curiosity, cooperation, long-term identity, and player history rather than temporary spectacle.

Talvaren Studios is also creating the TerrainVale Suite, a set of Unity terrain and worldbuilding tools used in the production of Talvaren.
