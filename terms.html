Talvaren Terms and Community Rules

A shared world starts with respect for the people who explore it.

These terms explain the standards for using the Talvaren Studios website, sending feedback and participating in Talvaren and its studio-operated community spaces. They cover fair play, respectful communication, account safety and how to raise a concern. Questions can be sent to support@talvarenstudios.com.

Access and development

Talvaren is in active development. A public game download is not yet available. The Enter Talvaren page provides the current launch and access information, and Player Information covers the planned platform, content and preparation for play.

The current Download & Install page is a preview. Reading it, selecting its checkbox or pressing Download does not create a game account, place an order or record an agreement. Browsing the website and sending feedback do not reserve a place in a test or grant game access.

Development footage and descriptions show a game being made. Features, balance and presentation may change as development continues. Online features depend on the relevant services being available, and maintenance, outages or updates can interrupt access.

Playing fairly and protecting accounts

Play for personal enjoyment and follow the rules of the store or platform through which you obtain access. Keep your sign-in details secure. Do not use another person's account without permission, impersonate a player or staff member, or attempt to gain unauthorized access to an account or service.

Do not use cheats, bots or exploits to gain an unfair advantage, steal items or disrupt play. If you discover a bug or security issue, report it privately to support rather than distributing instructions that help others abuse it. Include only the information needed to explain the problem, and do not send passwords or account tokens.

Community standards

These rules apply to in-game communication and Talvaren community spaces that we operate. Discussions can be spirited, and criticism is welcome. Keep the disagreement about the idea, the design or the experience rather than turning it into abuse of another person.

  • Respect other people. Do not threaten, harass, stalk or target people with hateful abuse.
  • Protect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information or share their private messages without permission.
  • Keep participation honest. Do not scam, impersonate staff, spread malicious software, spam or attempt to break into accounts or services.
  • Keep the community safe. Do not post illegal or sexually exploitative material, or solicit sexual content from minors. Follow any additional content rules displayed in the community space you are using.
  • Respect moderation. Follow moderator instructions and do not evade restrictions by using another account. Disagreement is allowed; deliberate disruption is not.

Reporting a problem

Send reports to support@talvarenstudios.com. Include the player name or relevant page, the approximate date and time, and a clear description of what happened. Relevant screenshots or message excerpts can help establish the context.

Remove unrelated personal information before sending. Do not retaliate, encourage others to target the person involved or post private information publicly. For an exploit or security issue, explain the problem privately rather than posting it in a public discussion.

Moderation and review

We may remove content or restrict communication or game access when rules are broken. Measures can include a warning, temporary restriction or permanent suspension, depending on the seriousness and repetition of the behavior. Serious threats, exploitation or account attacks may lead to immediate action.

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, contact support with the relevant notice and the information you would like considered. Explain the issue calmly and include the context that may have been missed. Do not send account passwords or another person's unrelated private information.

Purchases and consumer rights

The current Talvaren download page does not process purchases. Before paying for game access through a store or platform, read the price, what is included, any recurring billing terms and the seller's refund policy. For a payment problem, use the support channel of the seller that processed the transaction.

Nothing in these terms removes consumer rights that the law does not allow to be excluded.

Creative work and ownership

Talvaren's software, artwork, writing, music and other materials remain the property of their respective rights holders. Access to the game or website does not transfer ownership of those materials.

You are welcome to point others to the official website using Share Talvaren or a direct link. For questions about permission to reuse material, contact Talvaren Studios. Respect the rights of other creators when including images, writing or other material in feedback or community posts.

Privacy and contact

The Privacy Policy explains the website's hosting, feedback, support and media services, along with ways to make a privacy request. Do not put information in a public conversation that you would not want other people to see.

For help, questions about these terms or a community concern, email support@talvarenstudios.com. General suggestions can also be sent through Feedback & Support.

Thank you for helping make Talvaren a place where people can explore, disagree, learn and build stories together.
