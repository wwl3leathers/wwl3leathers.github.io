Controls & Key Bindings

In development

Find your footing before you set out.

Use this guide for Talvaren's current keyboard and mouse controls. The bindings below describe the development build and may change before release.

Last checked: November 10, 2026.

Fixed keys Movement & camera Actions Windows Change bindings

Fixed keys

Only H, Enter and Esc are fixed. These keys cannot be reassigned.

H
Hello. Greet a selected trainer within 2 m and open the supported dialogue. General greetings to players and other creatures are still in development.
Enter / Numpad Enter
Chat. Focus the selected chat channel, then attempt to send the draft. Chat delivery is not available in the current build. Enter or Send keeps your unsent text and reports that delivery is unavailable.
Esc
Menu and cancel. Open the menu, release the pointer or cancel the current interaction, depending on context.

Movement & camera

These are the current defaults, not fixed bindings. Your applied keybinding profile may be different.

W / S
Move forward / backward.
A / D
Strafe left / right.
Q / Right Arrow
Turn left / right on foot. These are configurable development defaults.
Left Shift / Right Shift
Hold to sprint. Right Shift is an alternate binding.
Space
Jump / swim up.
Left Ctrl
Descend / swim down.
Hold / drag LMB
Orbit the camera without turning your character. Also orbits the view while at the helm.
Hold RMB
Steer your character and view. Right mouse alone does not move you forward. At a cannon, it controls aiming.
Hold LMB + RMB
Move forward while steering.
Wheel scroll
Zoom the camera. Mouse movement and scrolling are camera controls, separate from button rebinding.

Mouse labels: LMB is the left mouse button, RMB is the right mouse button, and MMB is pressing the scroll wheel.

Actions & combat

These defaults can be reassigned. Numbered keys use the top number row shown in the illustration.

E
Interact / leave a cannon. General world interaction is still being developed.
F
Helm interaction / leave the helm.
Tab
Select a combat target.
1
Primary action slot 1, normally Auto Attack.
2 3 4 5
Primary action slots 2–5, used for your class rotation.
6
Primary action slot 6, used for the ranged-action position.
Release LMB
World-click interaction. A click is different from holding and dragging to orbit the camera.
Hold MMB
Block.
Left Alt + direction
Dodge while holding a movement direction. Temporary review binding: the final default has not been chosen.

Follow the action shown on your hotbar. Slot availability depends on your class, equipment and the current implementation. Unavailable actions do not execute.

At the helm: W / S / A / D control forward / reverse / port / starboard. Hold S to slow through a stop into reverse. Space is not a ship-stop key, and Shift does not activate With The Wind. Station hotbars show their own contextual actions.

Windows

J
Open / close the Journal. Remappable default.
I
Inventory. Configurable development default.

Change your bindings

Open Esc → Interface → Keybindings. Make your changes, then choose Apply to save them. Cancel leaves your current controls unchanged.

  • WASD and the top-row 1–6 keys are remappable defaults even though they are highlighted in the image.
  • H, Enter, Numpad Enter and Esc remain reserved.
  • If a change displaces another action, assign that action a replacement before applying. Conflicting or incomplete bindings cannot be applied.
  • Profiles are saved locally on the device, not to an account server.
  • The current editor supports left, right and middle mouse buttons, plus Mouse Back and Mouse Forward. Back and Forward have no default action. The illustration's four optional side-button labels do not mean four additional buttons have assigned or supported actions.

Still being decided

Final bindings for general world interaction, auxiliary-hotbar slots and cycling, fishing reactions and loot-specific interaction are not yet confirmed. No final defaults are listed here for those actions.
