Talvaren Player Information

Welcome to Talvaren, a fantasy MMORPG about the road your choices create.

A well-prepared expedition, a hard-earned skill, a companion who knows the way home. Talvaren is being built for players who want those things to matter. Your character's strengths, limitations and growing knowledge shape how you meet the world, and the people beside you can change what is possible.

This guide introduces the game, explains its current availability, and brings together the practical information you need before playing.

A world worth learning

Talvaren's design puts exploration, character identity and cooperation at the heart of the journey. Intelligence is connected to literacy, Wisdom to navigation, and preparation to survival. A difficult beginning is part of a character's story, with room to learn and grow.

Ten classes are planned, with adventuring and professional lives both central to the world:

  • Adventurers: Warrior, Rogue, Priest, Wizard and Necromancer.
  • Professionals: Outfitter, Jeweler, Provisioner, Scribe and Laborer.

Whether you are drawn to direct confrontation, making equipment, supplying an expedition or uncovering knowledge, there is a place for a character whose contribution matters to others. Explore Classes & Peoples to meet the inhabitants of Talvaren and discover the roles taking shape.

Release and access

Talvaren is in active development. A closed beta is expected in December, with more details to follow. A public game download is not yet available.

Enter Talvaren is the official starting point for launch and access information. Its Launch & Servers section brings together the current announcements. The Download & Install page is available to read, but its Download button does not yet provide a game file or record an agreement.

Development images and videos offer a look at the world as it is being made. Features, balance and presentation may change during development.

Platform and computer requirements

The current build targets 64-bit Windows PC. Minimum and recommended hardware specifications have not yet been published. Steam Deck and Steam Machine compatibility has not been tested.

Before choosing hardware specifically for Talvaren, check the published requirements for the build you intend to play. Development footage is not a performance benchmark for your computer.

Content information

Talvaren contains suggested and animated violence, occasional language, and depictions of drinking and smoking. It does not include graphic gore.

Parents and guardians can use this information, together with the game's available media, to decide whether its themes are suitable for their family.

Photosensitivity and comfortable play

Flashing lights, moving patterns and other visual effects in video games can trigger seizures in some people, including people with no previous history of seizures or epilepsy.

Stop playing immediately and seek medical advice if you experience dizziness, altered vision, muscle twitching, confusion, loss of awareness or a seizure. If you have a history of seizures or epilepsy, consult a medical professional before playing.

Play in a well-lit room, sit a comfortable distance from the screen and take regular breaks. Avoid playing when tired. Visual settings may reduce discomfort, but do not guarantee that a game is safe for everyone with photosensitivity.

Sharing the journey

Talvaren's community is a place to compare ideas, ask questions and share discoveries. Treat other players with respect, protect their privacy and keep competition fair. The Terms and Community Rules explain these expectations and how to report a concern.

You can share the official website with friends using Share Talvaren on the Enter Talvaren page. For a closer look before release, visit the Media section or explore the History and Places of the world.

Help and bug reports

For help with Talvaren, email support@talvarenstudios.com. Ideas, questions and website feedback can also be sent through Feedback & Support.

A useful bug report explains what you were doing, what you expected, what happened instead and whether the problem can be repeated. Include the game version and any error message. For a performance problem, add your operating system, processor, memory and graphics card.

A screenshot or relevant log can help explain a problem. Remove passwords, account tokens and unrelated personal information before sending, and never include payment-card details. The Privacy Policy explains how the website and its contact services handle information.

Thank you for taking an interest in Talvaren. We hope you find a road worth following.
