Talvaren Privacy Policy

Understand what happens to your information when you visit Talvaren Studios or get in touch.

This policy covers the Talvaren Studios website, its feedback form, support correspondence and the media services used on the site. You can explore the public website without creating a Talvaren account. Talvaren is still in development, and the current Download & Install page does not register players, take payment or record acceptance of an agreement.

When you contact us

When you send feedback, we receive the message and the details you choose to include. The website form asks for a name, email address, subject category and message. The name and email fields are optional; the message is required. If you want a reply, include an email address where you can be reached.

Messages submitted through the form pass through FormSubmit and are delivered to Talvaren Studios at walter@talvarenstudios.com. The form uses spam-prevention measures and may present a CAPTCHA check. FormSubmit's handling of submissions is described in its privacy information.

You can also email support@talvarenstudios.com directly. Email correspondence includes your sender address, message and any attachments you send, and passes through the email services involved in delivery.

Information in your message helps us understand your question, investigate a reported problem and respond. A bug report may include your game version, computer specifications, error messages or screenshots. Please send only the details relevant to the issue. Do not send passwords, authentication codes, account tokens or payment-card information.

Browsing and website hosting

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. Loading a page or file sends a request to the hosting service, which receives technical information such as your IP address and browser request details. GitHub describes its handling of personal information, including service logs and security information, in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The website also stores copies of pages, images and other site files in your browser's cache. This supports loading the site and showing previously cached content when a connection is unavailable. These cached website files are stored on your device. You can remove them through your browser's site-data controls; doing so may remove offline access until the pages are loaded again.

Videos and other external services

Some media on the site uses YouTube. Video thumbnail images may load from YouTube's image servers when you open a media page. Selecting a YouTube video opens an embedded player using YouTube's privacy-enhanced domain, youtube-nocookie.com. These connections give the provider technical information needed to serve the image or video.

Privacy-enhanced playback does not mean that no information is exchanged with YouTube. Google's Privacy Policy explains its data practices and available controls. You can choose not to open an embedded video and can manage cookies and other site data in your browser.

Links to other websites take you to services operated by their respective providers. Their privacy policies and account settings apply to information you give them. A link from Talvaren Studios does not make those services part of this website.

Share Talvaren

The Share Talvaren button uses your device's sharing menu when available, or helps you copy the current page's link. You choose whether to share it and which app or recipient to use. Any app you select handles the resulting message under its own privacy practices.

Your choices and privacy questions

You can browse without sending feedback, leave the optional name and email fields blank, choose not to open external media, and clear this website's stored files using your browser settings. Clearing browser data does not delete a message you have already sent by email or through the feedback form.

For questions about information you have sent to Talvaren Studios, or to request access, correction or deletion, contact support@talvarenstudios.com with “Privacy request” in the subject. Identify the correspondence or information concerned so the request can be located. Do not send your password. Verification may be needed to protect the information from disclosure to someone else.

Your rights depend on where you live and may include access, correction, deletion, portability, objection or restrictions on certain uses of personal information. Where applicable, you may also raise a concern with your local data protection authority. For requests about information held independently by GitHub, FormSubmit, Google or your email provider, contact that provider directly.

Parents and guardians

If you are concerned that a child has included personal information in a message to Talvaren Studios, contact support with enough detail to identify the message. Avoid including additional information about the child that is not needed to explain the concern.

Keeping your information safe

Use the website's HTTPS address and take care with the information you include in messages or attachments. No online service or email channel can guarantee absolute security. If a message contains a password or other account secret, change it through the relevant service rather than sending it again in a support request.
